## Full description (2232/4000)

```text
Note: the app interface is in Vietnamese. Tabs, note names, chord names and the music itself work the same in any language.

Sổ Tab Đàn Tranh – Đàn Bầu is a songbook and practice partner for the Vietnamese zither đàn tranh (16/17 strings, plus 19/21/22-string models) and the monochord đàn bầu.

MADE FOR ĐÀN TRANH AND ĐÀN BẦU
• Đàn tranh: string-number tabs with bend (nhấn) marks and a picture of the strings under each note; northern or alternate tuning, any key.
• Đàn bầu: harmonic-point tabs (½, ⅓, ¼…) with lever bends up or down, and a picture of the string showing where to touch.
• Harmonics trainer: the app listens to each pluck and tells you if you hit the harmonic, the open string or the wrong point.
• Lever and bend trainers: a pitch meter shows too little, right or too much.
• Tuner for tuning the strings.

PLAY ALONG
• Play songs with a backing track: many rhythm styles (ballad, waltz, rock, bossa…), chord accompaniment, count-in, tempo control and loop for hard passages.
• Auto-scroll: the tab scrolls by itself at your own tempo while you play, no hands needed.
• Karaoke: lyrics follow the music, pitch scoring through the microphone, record and listen back.
• Wait mode: the app only moves on when you play the right note, and scores each run.
• Tuner: see which note you are playing and how far off it is.

ADD NEW SONGS
• Sheet music scanner: take a photo or pick a PDF of printed music; notes, repeats and chord names are read on the phone.
• Open MIDI, karaoke .kar and MusicXML files.
• Paste tabs or lyrics with chords copied from the web.
• Share a song with friends through a single link.

PRACTISE EVERY DAY
• Lessons from the basics, with playable exercises.
• Call and response: the app plays a short phrase, you play it back, and it checks each note.
• Rhythm trainer: play along with a metronome and see which notes were early or late.
• Practice log: time practised, day streak and a suggested routine for today.

LIGHT AND PRIVATE
• Works offline, no account, no ads.
• Songs, recordings and settings stay on your device; export a backup file any time.
• AI features (reading a photo, recalling a song by name) are optional and only run when you tap them.
• Sample songs are public domain.
```
